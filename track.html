How shipping works

  • Handmade pieces take roughly 3–4 weeks to complete after payment
  • Once finished, your order is handed to Pathao for pickup and delivery
  • Tracking updates run through NCM (Nepal Can Move)
  • You'll get your tracking number by WhatsApp as soon as it ships
  • Prefer to order directly? Use our Pathao storefront
