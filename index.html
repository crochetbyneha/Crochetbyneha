About the studio

A cozy shop run by two people, one hook.

Crochetbyneh.a is a small handmade studio started by Neha, who hand-makes every single piece with soft, high-quality yarn — plushies, wearables, and home decor, one loop at a time. Her partner Sabin runs the shop end: orders, tracking, and every message that comes through.

Neha's craft

  • Handmakes every piece with care
  • Uses soft, high-quality yarn
  • Plushies, wearables & home decor

Sabin & the shop

  • Manages the website & orders
  • Helps track packages
  • Answers your messages

Why shop here

  • Every item is unique
  • Support a small local dream
  • Custom orders welcome
Please note: we keep zero initial stock. Every order is made after payment, and handmade pieces take roughly 3–4 weeks depending on the item. Follow along on TikTok or Instagram for progress updates.
