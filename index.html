Custom Orders

Have something in mind? Let's make it.

Most custom pieces are ready in 2–4 weeks depending on size and queue. Here's how it works, start to finish.

01

Send your idea

A photo, color, or rough sketch is enough to start the conversation.

02

Get a quote

You'll get a price and estimated timeline before anything is booked.

03

Approve a swatch

A small yarn swatch confirms color and texture before the full piece begins.

04

It's hooked by hand

Every stitch is made one at a time — you'll get progress photos along the way.

05

Packed & shipped

Wrapped with care and shipped with tracking, ready to unbox.

The Maker

Hi, I'm Neha.

What started as a way to unwind after work turned into a hook, a growing yarn stash, and eventually crochetbyneha. I make everything myself, in small batches, usually with tea going cold beside me.

I care about where my yarn comes from and how long a piece will last — nothing here is rushed, and nothing leaves my hands until I'd keep it myself.

If you don't see exactly what you want in the shop, that's usually where the best pieces start. Just ask.

— Neha
