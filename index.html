The studio

Two people, one craft.

Crochetbyneh.a is a cozy little studio run by a couple. Neha hand-makes every single piece with soft, high-quality yarn — plushies, wearables, and home decor, each one shaped with care and detail. Sabin runs the shop side, keeping orders, tracking, and questions moving smoothly so the pieces reach you without a hitch.

Made to order: we keep zero stock on purpose. Every piece is started only after payment, so please allow 3–4 weeks depending on the item. Follow along on TikTok or Instagram @crochetby.neha for progress and new drops.
